# Brasil's Information
Unless stated otherwise, the Brasil's consulates generally **do not** disclose the candidate's scores.
As stated in the [guidelines]({siteConfig.guidelines}), they also do not inform the reason for rejection.

The information in this page contains data from the 2026/2027 scholarship, and is subject to change.

## Belém
**Visit the [MEXT Belém Website]({embassies.belem}) to read the available information first and foremost.**

The interview is composed of five people in total. Two of them are MEXT alumni, there is also the two consuls, and a management lady.
All interviewers speak English. The consuls are Japanese.

They pay close attention to each candidate, respond very well, and are communicative.

Interview results are provided within a maximum of one month after the interview date.
Also, written exam results are sent via email the day after the exam.

As for the 2026/2027 process, `6x STC` and `6x UG` students applied for the scholarship.

São Paulo and Distrito federal used to directly recommend successful candidates. That means that passing the interview equates to passing the first screening.

However, other consulates would first forward the candidates who passed their interviews to the embassy. They would then recommend a specific number of these candidates, that number not necessarily representing the maximum possible, but rather the amount of individuals the embassy deemed likely to pass the second screening. Since rejection is still possible at this stage, passing the national phase effectively equates to passing the first screening.

<Note>
A specific feature of the graduate selection process is that—for São Paulo, and specifically up to the MEXT 2026 cycle—there was a waiting list system. Under this system, a maximum of one candidate from São Paulo who was not selected as the region's primary nominee was placed on the national waiting list; this meant that if a candidate selected at the national level declined the scholarship, the person on the waiting list would automatically be recommended.
</Note>
