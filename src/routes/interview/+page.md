# MEXT Interview
The interview stage is where you show your interest in the scholarship in detail. It typically lasts 10-15 minutes. 
This section is brought to you by `@hotcyanpepper`.

<Warning>
    This section covers only the questions normally asked in the UG, STC, and CoT categories.
    PG and other categories feature different interview styles and questions. Those will be added later. 
</Warning>

<Note>
    The questions shown here are likely harder than the questions you will be asked in the interview.
</Note>

## Question list:
1. Introduce yourself in English and Japanese (自己紹介).

2. What did you do at school besides academics?

3. What does your family think about you applying for this scholarship?

4. What do you want to explore and learn during your stay in Japan?
    - What besides your field?
    - What besides the Japanese Language/Culture?

5. What sparked your interest in Japanese culture?

6. Is there a specific place you want to visit during your stay in Japan?

7. Have you ever lived alone before?
    - Have you ever lived in another city/country?
    - Have you ever been abroad?
    - Have you ever traveled to Asia before?
    - Have you ever been to Japan before?

8. Do you feel confident that you could learn Japanese?
    - Do you think you can engage in University lectures without issues?

9. What are your plans to improve your Japanese skills before coming to Japan?

10. What do you know about the relationship between Japan and your country?
    - What role can you play in strengthening it?

11. Do you think you will be able to communicate and create bonds with Japanese people?
    - Do you already have Japanese friends?

12. How did you discover the MEXT scholarship?

13. What will you do after you graduate in Japan?
    - Are you going to continue working in Japan, or do you plan to return to your home country?
    - Do you plan on moving anywhere else?

14. Where do you see yourself in 10 years if:
    - You get the scholarship?
    - You don't get the scholarship? i.e. What are your backup plans?

15. What will be the most difficult and the easiest things you will have to face in Japan?

16. Tell us about your hobbies.

17. What is your purpose in going to Japan, from your own perspective, from Japan’s perspective, and from [your home country]’s perspective

## Field specific:
1. Why do you want to study ____?

2. Why do you want to study ____ in Japan specifically?
    - In USA and Europe, ____ is more well established. Why not study there?

3. Why ____ instead of another subfield inside your main field?
    - e.g Why do you want to study Particle Physics instead of other astrophysics or quantum physics?
    - e.g Why do you want to study Electrical engineering instead of Mechanical engineering?

4. If you were given the opportunity to specialize and research one specific theory of ____, what would that be and why?
